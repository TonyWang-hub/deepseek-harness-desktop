# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

[English](#english) | [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent while discussing ideas, respect different backgrounds and experience levels, and focus criticism on work rather than people.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable in repository spaces or project-related communication.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. For ordinary moderation concerns, open a repository discussion. For a confidential report, use [private reporting](https://github.com/TonyWang-hub/deepseek-harness-desktop/security/advisories/new) and prefix the title with `[conduct]`. Reports will be reviewed without retaliation, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is required for safety or law.

## 简体中文

我们致力于建立尊重且无骚扰的社区。请保持建设性沟通，讨论观点时假定对方善意，尊重不同背景和经验水平，并将批评聚焦于工作而非个人。

不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视、性化关注、公开他人隐私信息、故意扰乱以及反复的人身攻击。该要求适用于仓库空间和与项目相关的沟通。

为保护社区，维护者可编辑或删除内容、锁定讨论或限制参与。一般管理问题可在仓库 Discussion 中提出；需要保密时，请使用 [私密报告](https://github.com/TonyWang-hub/deepseek-harness-desktop/security/advisories/new)，并在标题前加 `[conduct]`。报告不会导致打击报复；如安全或法律要求披露，则无法承诺绝对保密。
